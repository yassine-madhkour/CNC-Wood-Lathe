# CNC Wood Lathe — Design and Manufacturing

## Project Overview

This final-year project focuses on the mechanical design and manufacturing of a CNC wood lathe.

The objective is to develop a machine capable of performing wood-turning operations through controlled movements along two axes (X and Z).

## Mechanical Design

- Study and design of the machine structure.
- Selection of mechanical components and materials.
- Design of the machine frame and axis support systems.
- Integration of linear guidance and transmission mechanisms.

## Manufacturing and Assembly

- Cutting and preparation of steel profiles.
- Drilling and welding of structural components.
- Manufacturing and assembly of the machine frame.
- Installation and alignment of mechanical components.

## Technical Skills

- Mechanical Design
- CNC Technology
- Metal Fabrication
- Welding and Assembly
- Mechanical Manufacturing

## Project Documentation

This repository is intended to present the project's mechanical design, manufacturing process, technical drawings and final results.
